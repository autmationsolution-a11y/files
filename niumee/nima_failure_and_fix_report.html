The finding behind most of the complaints

Clarissa's most frequent report all week was a version of "it's giving treatments which are not part of what she is asking". Chasing one missing keratin product uncovered why. 293 of the 975 services the clinic sells in Zenoti are absent from Nima's catalogue, about 30 per cent. All six CelluSmooth treatments are missing, which is exactly why Donna was told only a consultation exists. When the correct service is not there, the matcher reaches for the nearest wrong one. Aaron's massage was not really a matching bug. It was a missing-data bug wearing a matching bug's clothes.

Fixed

Each was reproduced from the live logs, corrected, then re-tested with the tester's original wording.

Touch-ups demanded a consultation that is not needed

27 services

What happened. Every touch-up carried the flag initial session first, which means the client must already have had the initial session. Nima read it as "book a consultation first" and sent existing clients away. Clarissa reported this once, for yearly eyebrows. It was happening on all 27 touch-up services.

Now. Nima now asks the client to confirm they have had the initial session, then books the treatment directly.

A price menu replaced real answers

4 of 17 cases

What happened. When a client added a provider or a time, the answer was overwritten by a bare numbered price list that silently dropped the qualifier. "Can I do tattoo removal in summer?" returned five prices and never mentioned summer.

Now. The rebuild now stands down whenever the client names a person or a date, so the real answer reaches them.

A keratin question returned a massage

verified

What happened. Aaron asked for "hair keratin and smoothing services" and was offered a Soothing Head, Neck & Shoulder Massage and two haircuts. The matcher bridged smoothing to soothing, and the bare word hair matches 47 rows.

Now. Keratin questions now resolve to keratin only.

A client could not change her appointment time

verified

What happened. Jessica reached the final step, then said three times that the slot no longer worked. Each time she got the same request for her email address. The only escape was the tester word skip, which no real client knows.

Now. Saying the time does not work now takes the client straight back to the available slots.

A staff member was told to seek urgent medical care

OPEN

What happened. Alessandro wrote that he wanted to reschedule because he had woken up with a fever. The symptom detector fired on the word fever and told him to seek urgent medical attention, and raised a false clinical alert to reception.

Now. Identified and reproduced. Not yet fixed.

A former staff member was still bookable

verified

What happened. Frima Dag-o had left, but remained in the nail technician list, the fact sheet, the name map and, critically, the availability roster, so she could still be offered for appointments.

Now. Removed from all five locations including the roster.

A staff member got no reply at all

11 OPEN

What happened. Aishah messaged three times and was silently ignored. Her number in the staff sheet was a 9-digit fragment because the export stripped country codes.

Now. Her real number is on the allowlist. 11 other staff are still affected by the same corruption and cannot use Nima.

Keratin menu was wrong and incomplete

verified

What happened. Cadiveu was still offered although it has been withdrawn, and the two treatments the salon actually promotes were absent entirely.

Now. Cadiveu hidden. QIQI Hair Controller Treatment (€300) and New Life Pro (€250) added with prices taken from live Zenoti, plus the rule that colour may be done with QIQI only.
